# The textile industry is going digital. Are you ready?

New EU regulations like ESPR and EPR demand full lifecycle responsibility. TOS13 is the fieldlab where textile, clothing, leather and footwear companies come together to **build, test, and validate** the digital infrastructure that makes circularity work — not as theory, but as a competitive edge.

<section class="phases" markdown="1">

## Three Phases. One Connected System.

Most digitalisation efforts fail because they tackle problems in isolation. TOS13 integrates the entire lifecycle into a single opportunity space — from data to production to end-of-life.

<div class="phase-grid" markdown="1">

<div class="phase-card" markdown="1">

### CLOE.ai — Encode

Turn regulatory obligations into operational power. CLOE.ai uses AI to transform Digital Product Passport data and ecosystem information into **actionable workflows** — so compliance becomes a launchpad, not a bottleneck.

</div>

<div class="phase-card" markdown="1">

### DISTILL — Materialise

Bridge the gap between data and making. DISTILL validates digital twins, digital sampling, and human-machine micro-factories that turn encoded data into **distributed production** — real products, real conditions, SME scale.

</div>

<div class="phase-card" markdown="1">

### ELEVATOR — Profile

Close the loop with intelligence. ELEVATOR deploys AI-driven evaluation at post-use thresholds, routing products toward their **highest-value next life** — resale, repair, remanufacture, or recycling.

</div>

</div>

<section class="details" markdown="1">

## The Fieldlab

TOS13 is a physical-digital fieldlab hosted at **Textile Campus Tilburg**, running from May 2026 through October 2027. Technologies are validated from TRL 3-4 to TRL 6-7 — bridging the gap between prototype and market-ready.

The project is led by **Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)** and backed by a consortium that spans the full textile lifecycle.

</section>
