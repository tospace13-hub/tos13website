<section class="section section--yellow" id="why-now">
<div class="container">
<h2>Why now</h2>
<p class="section-intro">Regulation, an ageing workforce and AI are hitting SMEs at the same time.</p>
<div class="card-grid card-grid--3">
<div class="benefit">
<h3>New EU rules</h3>
<p>The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR), with its Digital Product Passport (DPP), and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR, in Dutch UPV) demand product and material data that most SMEs do not yet collect.</p>
</div>
<div class="benefit">
<h3>Ageing workforce</h3>
<p>Production know-how lives in the heads and hands of technicians, and is retiring with them.</p>
</div>
<div class="benefit">
<h3>AI and digitalisation</h3>
<p>New tools are arriving, but they are built for companies with data teams and proprietary systems.</p>
</div>
</div>
<p class="section-note">Without shared knowledge, every SME faces these shifts alone.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="section section--white" id="about">
<div class="container">
<h2>What TOS13 is</h2>
<p class="section-intro">TOS13 builds a shared digital orchestration layer for the Dutch textile sector that works with people, machines and waste.</p>
<div class="prose">
<p>TOS13 is a digital-physical fieldlab at the <strong>Textile Campus Tilburg</strong>, part of the NewTexEco programme and anchored in the Brainport ecosystem. Companies, researchers and technology partners develop practical digital solutions and test them directly in real-world settings — starting from the concrete challenges of the companies themselves, not from a theoretical model.</p>
<p>TU/e leads the fieldlab using research through design: designing, developing, testing and improving. The demonstrators, digital tools, frameworks and business cases the fieldlab produces are made available to other organisations in the sector.</p>
</div>
<div class="question">
<span class="label">Central knowledge question</span>
<p>How can we use data to create a sector-level digital orchestration layer that helps Dutch textile SMEs create impact and profitability, and collaborate across design, product, material, production, use, reuse and end-of-life?</p>
</div>
<div class="stats stats--compact">
<div class="stat">

<p>work packages</p>
</div>
<div class="stat">
<span class="stat-value">1</span>
<p>integrated demonstrator</p>
</div>
<div class="stat">
<span class="stat-value">18</span>
<p>partner organisations</p>
</div>
</div>

<section class="section section--yellow" id="work-packages">
<div class="container">
<h2>Three work packages</h2>
<p class="section-intro">Three work packages follow textile data from design to production to second life, around three goals: connecting the sector, digitising analogue manufacturing and extending the useful life of textiles.</p>
<div class="wp-grid">
<article class="phase-card wp-card">
<div class="wp-main">
<span class="phase-number">01 · Connect</span>
<h3>Encode</h3>
<p class="wp-lead">Helping textile companies find each other and share DPP-ready data.</p>
<p>An orchestration platform that connects Dutch circular textile partners through the TELL data infrastructure. It maps partners to the steps of the Digital Product Passport and matches SME questions to the right knowledge and capacity.</p>
<dl class="wp-meta">
<dt>Leads</dt>
<dd>TU/e + Fashion Tech Farm</dd>
<dt>With</dt>
<dd>EnhanceThat, HvA, NewTexEco Partners, TextielLab Tilburg</dd>
</dl>
</div>
<div class="wp-side">
<div class="wp-usecase">
<span class="label">Use case</span>
<p>Collaboration starts with finding each other among 11,000 companies.</p>
</div>
<span class="label">What we deliver</span>
<ul class="wp-list">
<li>Ecosystem map and manufacturer/capacity database</li>
<li>DPP-step mapping and a minimum data-field set</li>
<li>Orchestration prototype, built in 3+ test cycles with SMEs</li>
<li>Physical demonstrator at the Textile Campus Tilburg</li>
</ul>
</div>
</article>
<article class="phase-card wp-card">
<div class="wp-main">
<span class="phase-number">02 · Make</span>
<h3>Distill</h3>
<p class="wp-lead">Turning the tacit knowledge of machines and operators into reusable data.</p>
<p>Data-enabled hybrid digital-physical manufacturing that plugs into digital supply chains and digital twins, making machines and production know-how digitally accessible for small-batch making.</p>
<dl class="wp-meta">
<dt>Leads</dt>
<dd>TU/e + Hollanders Printing Solutions</dd>
<dt>With</dt>
<dd>Vlisco, Vodde, TextielLab Tilburg, Saxion</dd>
</dl>
</div>
<div class="wp-side">
<div class="wp-usecase">
<span class="label">Use case</span>
<p>Vlisco: a modular textile/print prototyping machine for small-batch wax prints, built on production and digital-twin data.</p>
</div>
<span class="label">What we deliver</span>
<ul class="wp-list">
<li>Tested design-to-production scenarios with SME partners</li>
<li>Data-enabled machine configurations with telemetry</li>
<li>Shop-floor capture of technician and operator knowledge</li>
<li>Workflow template for small-batch, adaptive production</li>
</ul>
</div>
</article>
<article class="phase-card wp-card">
<div class="wp-main">
<span class="phase-number">03 · Retain value</span>
<h3>Elevate</h3>
<p class="wp-lead">Routing every used garment to its highest-value next life.</p>
<p>A traffic-control system for value retention: AI-supported assessment decides between repair, reuse, resale, upcycling and recycling.</p>
<dl class="wp-meta">
<dt>Leads</dt>
<dd>TU/e + ValueSort.ai</dd>
<dt>With</dt>
<dd>United Repair Centre, Stichting TexPlus, NXP Semiconductors, Saxion, ArtEZ, VNYX</dd>
</dl>
</div>
<div class="wp-side">
<div class="wp-usecase">
<span class="label">Use case</span>
<p>United Repair Centre: automated intake and damage assessment from garment photos, smart job routing and before/after quality control.</p>
</div>
<span class="label">What we deliver</span>
<ul class="wp-list">
<li>Intake and assessment data protocol for 5+ garment types</li>
<li>Labelled image dataset for damage classification</li>
<li>Validated routing criteria across the R-ladder</li>
<li>Evidence protocols for EPR reporting</li>
</ul>
</div>
</article>
</div>
<p class="section-note">Post-use data feeds back into design and production.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="section section--white" id="origins">
<div class="container">
<h2>From showroom to fieldlab</h2>
<p class="section-intro">TOS13 builds upon WoTO and NewTexEco: from a showroom, to a network, to a national fieldlab.</p>
<ol class="flow">
<li>
<span class="flow-step">01 · WoTO — the showroom</span>
<p>BYBORRE's Window of Textile Opportunities: machine makers, suppliers, universities and creators working side by side.</p>
<span class="flow-meta">Started by BYBORRE</span>
</li>
<li>
<span class="flow-step">02 · NewTexEco — the network</span>
<p>A national textile ecosystem. Its TELL infrastructure identifies who the Dutch textile companies are and where they sit in the chain.</p>
<span class="flow-meta">Started by ArtEZ, HvA/AMFI and Saxion</span>
</li>
<li>
<span class="flow-step">03 · TOS13 — the fieldlab</span>
<p>The original plan outgrew its scope and became something bigger: an orchestration system for the Dutch textile sector.</p>
<span class="flow-meta">Led by TU/e</span>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
</section>
