## Welcome

Textile Opportunity Space 13 (TOS13) is a textile ecosystem digital fieldlab dedicated to advancing innovation in the textile industry through collaborative research, open knowledge sharing, and digital tooling.

## What We Do

We bring together researchers, designers, and industry partners to explore sustainable textile futures — connecting circular economy practices with digital technologies.

## Get Involved

TOS13 is an open fieldlab. If you are interested in collaborating or learning more, reach out to us.
